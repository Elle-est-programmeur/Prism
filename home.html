// three novel engines

What makes PRISM
fundamentally different.

Not just a RAG chatbot over your docs. Three layers that no existing tool has built.

Engine 01 · Unique
Staleness Verifier
After generating an answer, a second LLM call checks whether that answer still matches the current version of your policy document. If the policy changed, PRISM flags the contradiction with a side-by-side diff — before it reaches the customer. Nobody else has built this.
Engine 02 · Novel
Ontology Graph
A control concept graph maps every question — across SOC 2, ISO 27001, CAIQ, and vendor DDQs — to the same underlying security control node. "Encrypt data at rest?" and "Describe your data encryption controls" are the same node. Answers are reused across frameworks automatically.
Engine 03 · Smart
Buyer-Aware Synthesis
PRISM reads the questionnaire's framing — NIST lens, GDPR lens, procurement lens — and adjusts the answer's depth and terminology. A technical CAIQ buyer gets precise control-level language. A procurement buyer gets plain English. Same underlying answer, right presentation.
Engine 04 · Learning
Corrections Loop
When a human approves or edits an amber or red answer, the corrected version is stored back to the control node in the ontology graph. The next questionnaire with the same control gets the corrected answer first. PRISM improves with every review cycle.
Staleness verifier — live example
what the answer says
"Security logs are retained for 6 months in accordance with our data retention policy, last updated Q2 2024."
what the current policy says
"All security and access logs shall be retained for a minimum of 12 months as required by updated ISO 27001 controls, effective Jan 2025."
PRISM caught this. Every existing tool would have sent "6 months" to your customer — a compliance inaccuracy that could cost you the deal or a certification.
